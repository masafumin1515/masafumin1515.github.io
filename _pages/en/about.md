## Bio

I am a Ph.D. student at the Graduate School of Engineering Science, The University of Osaka.<br>
Since my master's course, I have been working in the laboratory of [Prof. Kazuyasu Sugiyama](https://rd.iai.osaka-u.ac.jp/ja/4f3be6b665b51b35.html).<br>
During my undergraduate studies, I worked on turbulence under the supervision of [Prof. Susumu Goto](https://fm.me.es.osaka-u.ac.jp/goto/index.html) and [Dr. Yutaro Motoori](https://ymotoori.github.io/).<br>
My research fields are fluid mechanics and microfluidics. I currently study particle migration in microchannels.<br>
Outside research, I enjoy working out at the gym🏋️‍♀️
